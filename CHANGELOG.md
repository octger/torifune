## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-31

### Added

- URL and playlist analysis with selectable download formats
- persistent concurrent download queue with pause, resume, cancel, and retry
- video preview, range selection, waveform comparison, and cache management
- two-pass loudness normalization and FHD post-processing
- explicit-consent dependency download with SHA-256 verification
- portable mode, diagnostic console, and sanitized log display
- reproducible win-x64 release packaging with checksums and license notices
